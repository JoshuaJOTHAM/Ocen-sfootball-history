🏛️ Origins & Early Development

How a simple leather ball evolved into the world's greatest sport.

200 BCE – 200 CE

Cuju & Ancient Ball Games

Recognized by FIFA as the earliest form of football, **Cuju** (蹴鞠) was played in Han Dynasty China. Soldiers kicked a leather ball filled with feathers into a small net mounted on bamboo canes without using hands.

12th – 18th Century

Medieval Mob Football

In Shrovetide England, entire neighboring villages competed to drag an inflated pig's bladder to designated landmarks miles apart. The matches were notorious for wild riots, leading King Edward III to ban the sport in 1365.

October 26, 1863

The 1863 FA Founding

Representatives from 11 London public schools gathered at **Freemasons' Tavern** to establish standard rules. This historic split separated Association Football ("Soccer") from Rugby, creating the unified sport we know today.

November 30, 1872

First International: Scotland vs England

Played at Hamilton Crescent in Glasgow before 4,000 spectators. The match ended 0-0, but debuted Scotland's revolutionary "passing game" against England's individual dribbling style.

1890s – 1910s

Global Expansion: South America & Europe

British sailors, railway engineers, and students brought football to Argentina (Alexander Watson Hutton) and Brazil (Charles Miller), laying the groundwork for South America's eventual global dominance.

May 21, 1904

Founding of FIFA in Paris

Seven European nations (France, Belgium, Denmark, Netherlands, Spain, Sweden, Switzerland) founded the *Fédération Internationale de Football Association* to govern international competitions.
